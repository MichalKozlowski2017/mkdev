# Privacy Policy — Glut

Last updated: November 2026

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Glut mobile game ("Glut", "we", "us") processes data.

Controller contact for privacy matters: **[mkdev.appslab@gmail.com](mailto:mkdev.appslab@gmail.com)**

## 2. What Glut Does

Glut is an arcade climbing game. It does not require an account, shows no ads and has no in-app purchases.

## 3. Data We Process

### 3.1 Data stored locally on your device

Glut stores the following data only on your device to make the game work:

- game progress (best height, candies, daily challenge results, achievements, wardrobe items),
- settings (music, sounds, vibration, language),
- your choice about anonymous gameplay statistics,
- an anonymous, randomly generated install identifier (only if gameplay statistics are enabled).

This data is removed when you uninstall the game.

### 3.2 Leaderboards (Game Center / Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center (iOS) or Google Play Games (Android), Glut submits your best height and daily challenge results there, so they can be shown on leaderboards together with your nickname from that service. This data is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it. Without signing in, leaderboards are unavailable and the game works normally. You can remove your scores in the Game Center or Google Play Games settings.

### 3.3 Anonymous gameplay statistics (optional)

To balance difficulty and fix problems, Glut can send anonymous gameplay statistics to our own server, only if you allow it. They include:

- game events such as run start and end, height reached, run time, cause of the run ending, world reached, candies collected, achievements unlocked and wardrobe purchases,
- technical data: app version, platform (iOS / Android), operating system version, device model class, language and frame rate,
- the anonymous install identifier described above.

Statistics do not include your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or advertising identifiers. IP addresses are used only to deliver requests and are not stored with the statistics.

- Statistics are optional and sent only after you agree.
- You can change your choice anytime in **Settings**.

## 4. Advertising

Glut shows no ads and does not use your device's advertising identifier.

## 5. Purposes and Legal Bases

We process data to:

- provide the game and its features (local progress, leaderboards),
- improve the game (anonymous statistics, where you agreed).

Depending on your jurisdiction, legal bases may include:

- performance of a service (game features),
- consent (optional statistics).

## 6. Data Retention

- Local game data remains on your device until you uninstall the game.
- Anonymous statistics are kept for up to 24 months and are then deleted or aggregated.
- Leaderboard data retention is governed by Apple and Google.

## 7. Data Sharing and Sales

We do not sell data. Anonymous statistics are stored with our hosting providers (Vercel and its database partner) and are not shared with anyone else.

## 8. International Transfers

Some service providers may process data outside your country/region (including outside the EEA, where applicable).  
Such processing is governed by those providers' contractual and legal safeguards.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

- access data,
- correct data,
- delete data,
- restrict or object to processing,
- withdraw consent (for statistics),
- lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Because statistics are anonymous, we may need the install identifier shown in the game settings to find your data. Contact us at: **[mkdev.appslab@gmail.com](mailto:mkdev.appslab@gmail.com)**.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Glut is not directed to children under 13 (or the age threshold defined by applicable local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect game changes, legal requirements, or provider changes.  
The latest version is published at a stable URL linked from the game and its store pages.
