# Privacy Policy — Bystrzakowo

Last updated: November 2026

## 1. Who We Are

This Privacy Policy describes how the Bystrzakowo mobile app ("Bystrzakowo", "we", "us") handles data.

Controller contact for privacy matters: **[mkdev.appslab@gmail.com](mailto:mkdev.appslab@gmail.com)**

## 2. What Bystrzakowo Does

Bystrzakowo is an educational app for children aged 3 to 6 with voiced games about counting, letters, colors, shapes, emotions and logic. It works fully offline and does not require an account.

## 3. We Do Not Collect Data

Bystrzakowo does not collect, transmit or share any personal or usage data. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs, and it does not use the device's advertising identifier. It does not connect to our servers.

## 4. Data Stored Only on the Device

To make the app work, Bystrzakowo stores the following only on the device:

- game progress (difficulty levels, collected stickers, the record of solved tasks used for the parent report),
- the child's first name, if a parent enters it in the parent zone (optional, used only on the report and the postcard),
- the child's creations (coloring pages and sticker pictures),
- settings (music, play time limit, language) and the trial status.

This data never leaves the device unless a parent chooses to share a picture or a postcard (see section 5). It is deleted when the app is uninstalled, and a parent can reset progress at any time in the parent zone.

## 5. Sharing Pictures (Parent Zone Only)

In the parent zone, which is protected by a parental gate, a parent can save or send a child's picture or the weekly postcard using the device's system share sheet. The image is passed to the app or service the parent chooses (for example Photos, Messages or email); we do not receive it.

## 6. In-App Purchase

The full version is unlocked with a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play), behind a parental gate. Payment data is processed by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it. The app only checks with the store whether the purchase was made.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Bystrzakowo is designed for children. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the GDPR and the App Store Kids Category and Google Play Families Policy requirements, we do not collect personal information from children. Features intended for adults (the progress report, sharing, purchases and settings) are available only behind a parental gate.

## 8. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store any data on our side, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. All data stays on your device, and you can delete it by resetting progress in the parent zone or uninstalling the app. If you have any questions, contact us at **[mkdev.appslab@gmail.com](mailto:mkdev.appslab@gmail.com)**.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect app changes or legal requirements. The latest version is published at a stable URL linked from the app's store pages.
