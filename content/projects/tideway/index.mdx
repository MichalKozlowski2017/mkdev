**Tideway** is an automated news digest: it pulls public sources (RSS, Hacker News, and
more), writes short AI summaries, and publishes them as readable articles — so you get
“what’s hot today” without scrolling hundreds of links.

## Product

- **Categories:** Tech, IT, Games, AI, Sport, Finance — each with its own feed.
- **Article pages** with category/type labels, tags, reading time, and share actions.
- **Daily & weekly digests** that roll trends across all categories into one view.
- Clear labeling when content is **AI-generated** from public sources, with a path back
  to the originals.

## Engineering

- **Next.js 16** App Router with ISR-friendly publishing.
- **Neon Postgres** for articles, tags, and category data.
- **OpenAI** (or local Ollama) for summarization pipelines.
- Ingest via **RSS** and scheduled jobs; structured validation with **Zod**.

Built as a full content product end-to-end: ingestion → generation → publishing → reading UX.
