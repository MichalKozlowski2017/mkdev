Glut is a 2D arcade climber for iOS and Android. A sticky jelly slime launches itself
up a candy tower like a slingshot while syrup rises from below. It is the first game
from my small game workshop, a TypeScript monorepo for building mobile 2D games.

## Highlights

- **Simulation-first**: the game logic is plain TypeScript without the renderer, covered
  by unit tests and played by a bot that tunes difficulty across player profiles.
- **8 worlds** with their own mechanics, procedural music that reacts to every jump,
  and art generated with Flux 2 on a local GPU.
- **Daily challenge** with a seeded tower, verifiable replays and leaderboards through
  Game Center and Google Play Games.
- **Wardrobe and 38 achievements**, no ads and no in-app purchases at launch.
