Bystrzakowo (Smartyville in English) is a tablet-first learning app for preschoolers. Lusia
the fox and Basil the badger guide children through 21 games in four lands, and every
instruction is read aloud, so no reading is needed. It is the second app from my small game
workshop, built on the same TypeScript monorepo as [Glut](/projects/glut).

## Highlights

- **21 games in four lands**: puzzles, sorting, syllables, letter and number tracing, picture
  stories, coloring, stickers and melodies, with no losing and difficulty that adapts to the child.
- **Voiced in four languages** (Polish, English, German, Spanish): thousands of voice lines
  generated with text-to-speech and checked automatically with Whisper for pronunciation.
- **Parent zone** behind a parental gate: progress in plain words, charts for each pre-school
  skill, an album of the child's creations and a weekly postcard.
- **Plasticine-style art** generated with Flux 2 on a local GPU.
- **Kids category ready**: no ads, no analytics, no accounts, works offline and collects no data.
