energy-efficient construction (industrial halls, residential developments, design & build).

- Content managed in **Sanity** (studio-driven pages, projects, and offer content).
- Public site built as a static Vue/Gridsome front-end with a clean corporate layout.
- Sections for offer, technology, company profile, project portfolio (**Realizacje**),
  ZeroHouse, and contact.
