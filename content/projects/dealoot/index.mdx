Dealoot aggregates PC game discounts, free giveaways, Steam quality signals, price
history, and wishlist alerts — combining public deal APIs with Steam metadata so you
can compare prices and decide whether a game is worth buying.

## Highlights

- **Hot Deals** from CheapShark across 30+ stores, with value scores and Steam /
  Metacritic signals.
- **Free Games** — active giveaways from GamerPower.
- **Ranking** — SteamSpy-based top-rated lists with genre and review filters.
- **Price alerts** — wishlist tracking with target prices.
