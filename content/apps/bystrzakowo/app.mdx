Bystrzakowo is an educational mobile app (Android / iOS) for preschoolers aged 3 to 6. Outside Poland it is called Smartyville (English), Schlaudorf (German) and Listolandia (Spanish).
Lusia the fox and Basil the badger guide children through 21 games in four lands. Every
instruction is read aloud, so no reading is needed.

## Core features

- **Puzzle Meadow**: colors, first letters, animal mommies, pairs, shadows, odd one out,
  counting and emotions.
- **Basil's Workshop**: patterns, shapes, syllables, sorting, jigsaw puzzles and 24 picture
  stories to put in order.
- **Lusia's Path**: trace letters and numbers with a finger, dot-to-dot pictures and mazes.
- **Little Studio**: coloring pages, sticker pictures in seven landscapes and tunes to repeat.
- **Adaptive difficulty, no losing**: puzzles get harder as the child improves and easier after
  a mistake.
- **For parents**: a progress report in plain words with screen-free play ideas, progress charts
  for each pre-school skill (accuracy and answer time, by day or week), an album of the child's
  creations and a weekly postcard, behind a parental gate.
- **Languages**: Polish, English, German and Spanish.

No ads, no accounts, no internet connection needed and no data collection. Free for the first
3 days of play; afterwards Puzzle Meadow stays free and a one-time purchase unlocks everything.
