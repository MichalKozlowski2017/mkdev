Glut is a mobile arcade game (Android / iOS). You are a little jelly slime that sticks
to walls and ledges: pull back like a slingshot, let go, and climb as high as you can
before the rising syrup catches you.

## Core features

- **Slingshot controls**: drag, aim with the trajectory preview, release. Jump up to three
  times in mid-air when a leap falls short.
- **8 worlds**: from the crystal cave and the chocolate mine through cotton-candy clouds to
  the sugar nightmare. Each world adds a new twist: crumbling chocolate, bouncy jelly,
  pass-through clouds, conveyor belts.
- **Streaks and power-ups**: jump right after landing to keep your rhythm going, grab
  bubbles for shields, magnets, rockets and more.
- **Daily challenge**: the same tower for everyone, once a day.
- **Leaderboards**: best height and daily challenge via Game Center and Google Play Games.
- **Wardrobe and achievements**: hats, colours, trails and extras paid for with candies,
  38 achievements (some of them timed).

No account, no ads and no in-app purchases.
